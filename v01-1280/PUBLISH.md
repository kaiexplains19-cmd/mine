## Why the script is built the way it is
| Element | Where | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook in 3 seconds** | "All of humanity may once have fit inside one high school gym." | Impossible-sounding claim, instantly visual |
| **Open loop** | "…the climb nearly stopped." | Viewer needs the how and why |
| **Fact pops** | 1,280 · 100,000+ YEARS · 1980 · 1 PAIR · 1–2% · 8,000,000,000 | Something new on screen every few seconds |
| **Comment fuel #1** | "Not every scientist accepts the estimate" | Invites the "actually…" crowd to argue in the comments |
| **Comment fuel #2** | "If you've ever taken a DNA test, you might have seen it." | People post their Neanderthal % |
| **Subconscious CTA** | "Nobody made it alone. They stayed together. A bit like you, still here at the very end." | Ties sticking around to belonging; it primes following without asking |
| **Explicit question** | "Would YOU have made it into the 1,280? YES or NO" + on-screen pills | Lowest-effort comment possible |

Sources
• Hu et al. (2023), Science: ancient human population bottleneck (~1,280 breeding individuals, ~930,000–813,000 years ago). Some scientists dispute this estimate.

#history #prehistoric #humanevolution
