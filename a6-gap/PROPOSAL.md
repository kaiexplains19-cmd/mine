**Film:** "What If Only 1,280 Humans Were Left?" · **Runtime:** ~2:00 · **Ratio:** 9:16 vertical (Facebook Reels / TikTok / Shorts) · **Narration:** English, Gemini TTS · Puck (curious, exploratory)
**On-screen title (upper-left, pure white):** `WHAT IF ONLY` / `1,280 HUMANS WERE LEFT?`

## Why this topic
It combines the most proven premise in the niche (only a handful of humans left; can they restart humanity? Awaken's 5.3M-view video) with your own prehistory lane and real, citable science, told as a new story rather than a remake:
- **Hu et al. 2023, *Science*:** ancestors fell to ~1,280 breeding individuals for ~117,000 years, ~930,000–813,000 years ago. The estimate is contested, and the narration says so.
- **Black robin, 1980:** one breeding pair; all living birds descend from Old Blue.
- **Neanderthal interbreeding:** ~50,500–43,500 years ago (Sümer, Moorjani et al. 2024, *Science*); ~1–2% Neanderthal DNA in people with non-African ancestry.

## Story arc
**Hook:** all of humanity fits in one gym → **Assumption broken:** history isn't a steady climb → **Hidden mechanics:** 100,000 years on the edge, with climate, disaster and inbreeding risk → **Truth:** they made it, met Neanderthals, and we carry the proof → **Payoff:** "once, every person did" plus a question for the comments.

## Cast (story-specific; A6 drawing grammar for everyone)
All characters: compact matte-black round head, connected short black neck, small cream-white eye marks and a compact line mouth, simplified black hands and limbs, restrained cel shading, amber rim light, deep plum shadows.

1. **Tala**, a young ancestral woman, the lead. Rust-ochre hide wrap tied at one shoulder, braided plant-fibre belt with a small bone toggle, bare black feet. Carries a fire-hardened wooden spear.
2. **Oro**, the elder. A greyed antelope-hide cloak over the shoulders, a woven grass shawl, a long gnarled walking staff. Moves slowly and deliberately.
3. **Pip**, a child. A short tan hide tunic, and a carved wooden bird toy that stays in their hand in every shot they appear in.
4. **The modern viewer**, hook and ending only. A mustard-yellow bomber jacket over a white T-shirt, dark jeans. (Not the sample's red hoodie.)
5. **Brak**, a Neanderthal man. A broader, heavier build and a wider head silhouette, a thick dark fur cape, a stone hand-axe at the belt.
6. **Old Blue**, a small black robin with a coloured leg band. A non-speaking animal.
