### The rules this gives us
1. **"You" + one extreme change to your body, mind or world.** Every hit on both channels has it.
2. **A number in the title or thumbnail** (10x, 500, 2, 1,280, Day 28).
3. **Make sequels off a winner, but with a new angle, never a re-run** (Ifverse's 500 IQ series worked; Awaken's "1 Man" copy flopped at 3.3K).
4. **Generic sci-fi premises die** (2100, AI robot, alien planet).
5. **Long-form brings the reach; Shorts bring cheap discovery.** We need both: Shorts for subscribers and Facebook followers, long videos for YouTube watch hours.

## Weekly rhythm (built for monetization maths)
- **Every day:** one 45–60 s vertical Short (YouTube Shorts + Facebook Reels + TikTok). It's a cut-down of the week's long video or a mystery cliffhanger.
- **Two a week:** one 7–9 min long-form video (16:9) on a ★ idea. That's where YouTube watch hours come from: 4,000 hours is about 30,000 average-length views of a 8-minute video.
- **Sequel rule:** any video beating your channel average by 3× gets a **new-angle** follow-up within 7 days (like Ifverse's 500 IQ series), never a re-run.

## The honest maths for 30 days
- **YouTube full monetization:** 1,000 subs plus 4,000 watch hours (12 months), or 10M Shorts views (90 days). The **expanded tier** (500 subs plus 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views) unlocks fan funding first. Hitting either in 30 days needs at least one breakout video, like the ones both channels got early.
- **Facebook:** the most-cited figures are 5,000–10,000 followers plus 60,000–600,000 minutes viewed in 60 days. Check your exact targets in Professional Dashboard → Monetization.
- The plan maximizes the number of strong attempts, about 38 videos in 30 days, which is the only lever that reliably produces a breakout.
