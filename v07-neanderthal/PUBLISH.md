## Thumbnails: symbol-metaphor formula (the real Ifverse psychology)
Ifverse never shows the premise literally. It shows **one object everyone already understands**, which carries the idea and implies a consequence: a bullet caught = speed, a broken IQ gauge = genius, the Twin Towers + "09.11" = "he remembers the future". Pair it with a calm "I know something you don't" face and a short first-person line, and the viewer has to click to resolve it.

Run these three in **Test & Compare**:
1. `T-NEA-F.png`: **I DIDN'T / MOVE.** A charging **woolly rhino stopped dead by one calm palm**, with an impact burst, speed lines and a "2 TONS" tag. This is our "caught bullet": everyone knows you can't stop a rhino with your hand, so it reads instantly as *impossible strength*. It's also exactly what the first 20 seconds of the video deliver ("Everyone runs. You don't."), so the click is rewarded straight away. ← lead
2. `T-NEA-D.png`: **I NEVER / LEFT.** The Neanderthal smirking inside a **museum display case** labelled "EXTINCT". A museum exhibit means dead and gone, and he clearly isn't. *How?*
3. `T-NEA-E.png`: **HI, / COUSIN.** A **DNA test result: "NEANDERTHAL 2%"**, with the Neanderthal leaning in behind it. It implies he's *in you*.
