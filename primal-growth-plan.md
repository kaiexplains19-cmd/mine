# Primal (@primal_studios8): Diagnosis & Growth Plan

*Based on the Awaken teardown (`awaken-studios-analysis.md`). Data captured 13 Nov 2026.*

## Where you are

| | Primal | Awaken (benchmark) |
|---|---|---|
| Videos | 1 long, 0 Shorts | 5 long, 3 Shorts in 15 days |
| Your video | "Why DO Men Still act like Little Boys?" (Nov 11), **6 views** after 2 days | Hit: 5.3M in 24 days |
| Length / words | 10:00, ~1,900 words, **~190 wpm** | 7:00–8:15, ~1,200 words, **~155 wpm** |

Six views after two days doesn't tell you anything yet. New channels get very small test audiences at first, and Awaken's first video went viral, which is rare. The real problem is that **this video doesn't use the format that made Awaken grow.**

## What you're already doing well
- **Production quality:** clean, toon-shaded 3D with pastel sets and soft lighting. It's already at the level of Awaken's later videos.
- **Cold open:** "It's Saturday morning. Dave is 34… making laser noises" is a specific everyday moment followed by a gag. That's the right instinct.
- **Research:** 17 real sources in the description, and honest hedging ("tongue-in-cheek paper", "not an official diagnosis").
- **The ending:** an answer, then a callback joke ("It's called Kevin"), then a question for the comments.
- **Thumbnail:** 2 words, a yellow accent, a sweating main character, a crowd behind him. That's mostly right.

## What's holding it back

### 1. The topic is the type that flopped for Awaken
"Why do men act like boys" is an **observational explainer about a gender debate**. Awaken's closest equivalent (male vs female orgasm, a "his vs hers" explainer) got 18K. **All three of their hits were hypotheticals where the viewer is the main character and the survival of humanity is at stake.** Your video has no "you", no situation that changes the world, and no "what happens next?"

### 2. It's a list of facts, not a story
18 chapters that each make a new point. Nothing escalates, there's no time-skip, and nothing makes the viewer wait for a payoff. Awaken uses about 6 escalating beats inside one plot. At 190 words a minute, the facts also come too fast to land. Cut to about 1,200 words.

### 3. The title is weaker than it looks
- "Why DO Men Still act like Little Boys?" has inconsistent capitalisation, so it reads as a mistake rather than emphasis.
- A "Why" question invites the viewer to judge. A "What if you…" question puts them inside the story.

### 4. Your look is very close to Awaken's
Black round heads, large white oval eyes, identity shown only by clothes, cream-and-yellow heavy rounded thumbnail text, a sweat drop, a blue background with glow. Someone who has watched Awaken will likely see you as a copy, and that hurts trust and click-through. **Keep the formula, but give your characters one or two signature traits of their own.** Some options:
- Make the setting prehistoric: animal-skin clothes, wild hair, a single tooth, a bone necklace.
- Change the head shape or colour (e.g. warm brown clay heads, or a flint-grey stone texture), or use a different eye shape.
- Own a different colour palette, e.g. firelight orange plus cave teal, instead of Awaken's navy and yellow.
- Use a different thumbnail font and accent colour (e.g. chunky white text with an **orange** keyword).

### 5. The brand is inconsistent
The channel is named **Primal**, but the description says "**Origin** is an animation studio". The handle has a number in it (`primal_studios8`). It also promises "**new topics every day**", which you can't keep up at this quality. Awaken posts every 3 days.

### 6. You have no Shorts
Awaken uses Shorts as trailers (the full video compressed to 60 seconds, ending on the same comment question) and as history cliffhangers that end mid-sentence so they loop.

## Your advantage: the origins / prehistory angle
Awaken's lane is "end of humanity, modern day". Your channel description is about **where it all began**. That's the better lane for you: it's close enough to borrow the formula, and different enough that you don't read as a clone. Combine the two:

> **"You" + dropped into prehistory + survival stakes + the "who has babies with whom" curiosity + real science**

## Script spec for each video
```
7:00–8:00 · 1,100–1,250 words · ~155 wpm · 6–10 chapters · second person ("you")
0:00  Specific normal moment, then the world breaks. "Except you."
0:15  Stakes in one line plus a gag
0:30  Title card (exact title)
0:35  ACT 1  consequences, 5–6 stats, each with a picture you can imagine
1:45  "Easy. Except…"
2:00  ACT 2  3–4 escalating scenes: comedy / science / real case study
4:30  Time-skip ("Day 1… Winter 1… Year 10…") with on-screen badges
5:30  One sincere low beat
6:00  Real-world precedent (archaeology, a find, a study)
6:45  "So, could you? [2 words]. [nuance]."
7:15  Callback punchline, then a comment question, then link the companion video
```

## Thumbnail spec
- 2–3 words that never repeat the title, with a number wherever possible.
- One anxious main character against a world or crowd that wants something from them (a sabre-tooth, a tribe, a mammoth).
- Your own accent colour and font, not Awaken's cream-and-yellow.
- One focal face, readable at 160 px wide.

## Schedule
- **One long video every 3–4 days** for the first month (8 videos). That's enough attempts for one to break out.
- **Two Shorts for each long video:** one 60-second compressed trailer and one prehistory cliffhanger that ends mid-sentence.
- Update the channel description to say "new episodes twice a week" and settle on one name (Primal **or** Origin).

## What to check after each upload (YouTube Studio → Analytics)
1. **Impressions click-through rate** in the first 48 hours. Aim for **above 5%**. Below that, change the thumbnail first, then the title.
2. **Audience retention at 0:30.** Aim for **above 70%**. If people leave before the title card, the cold open is too slow.
3. **Average percentage viewed.** Aim for **above 45%** on a 7–8 minute video. If there's a big drop at one point, that scene is too much lecture and not enough story.
4. **Traffic sources:** growth comes when "Browse features" and "Suggested videos" start to outgrow search.
